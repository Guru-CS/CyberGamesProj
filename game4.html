Quick tutorial

How to play

  1. Move with WASD or arrows. Hold Shift to sprint.
  2. Aim the scope with the mouse (or I J K L). Hover a contact for a quick ID.
  3. Click a contact to launch a probe on them. Tag every suspicious part of their message before the probe runs out.
  4. Mark safe clears a genuine contact so the probe never flags them. Marking a scammer safe lets them slip away and costs a shield.
  5. Blast them with the Blast button or Space while the probe is on. Blasting a genuine contact costs a shield. Anyone who spots the probe starts chasing you.
