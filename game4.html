Quick tutorial

How to play

  1. Move with WASD or arrows. Hold Shift to sprint.
  2. Aim the lens with the mouse (or I J K L). Messages moving between people only show up inside the lens. Sweep it over a trace for a quick ID.
  3. Click a trace to launch a probe on the message. Tag every suspicious part before the probe runs out, or a security bot hunts you for 10 seconds. Sprint or dodge until it powers down.
  4. Mark safe clears a genuine sender so the lens stops tracing them. Marking a scammer safe lets them slip away and costs a shield.
  5. Report a scam to highlight the sender in red. Reporting a genuine contact costs a shield.
  6. Blast the highlighted scammer: follow the red arrow, get in range, put them in the lens, then click or press Space.
